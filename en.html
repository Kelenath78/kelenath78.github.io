The gameEvery run is different

Three acts, paths to choose, turn-based fights and a progression that makes you stronger with every attempt.

Deckbuilding

Attacks, hacks, implants and legendary cards: shape your deck fight after fight.

The megacity

Slums, factories, tower: a map to explore, “?” events, merchants and rival agents.

Evolution

XP earned in every fight and loot from every boss: upgrade your stats, talents and weapons between runs.

Everywhere

On computer and smartphone, installable as an app, playable offline. Your progress is saved, and synced with Google.

InstallPlay wherever you want

In the browser

  1. Click “Play”.
  2. That's it: nothing to install, your progress is saved on the device.
▶ PLAY

As an app (Android, iPhone)

  1. Open the game on your phone.
  2. Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → “Install app”.
  3. iPhone (Safari): Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. The game launches fullscreen, even offline.

Android app (recommended)

  1. Download the file on your Android phone.
  2. Open it and allow installation from your browser if Android asks.
  3. Sign in with Google in Options to find your progress from the website.
